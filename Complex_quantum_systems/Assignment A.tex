\section*{Statement}
Show that the spin singlet state \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} (\ket{01} - \ket{10})\) is rotationally invariant. That is, that it takes the same form
\[
    \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\ket{+}_\mathbf{u}\ket{-}_\mathbf{u}-\ket{-}_\mathbf{u}\ket{+}_\mathbf{u})
\]
for any direction \(\mathbf{u}\), the states \(\ket{+}_\mathbf{u}\) and \(\ket{-}_\mathbf{u}\) being eigenstates of \(\vec{\sigma} \cdot \mathbf{u}\).
